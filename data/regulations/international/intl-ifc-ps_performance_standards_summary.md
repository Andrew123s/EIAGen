# IFC Performance Standards on Environmental and Social Sustainability (2012) - Curated Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. INTERNATIONAL FRAMEWORK - NOT NATIONAL
> LAW. The Performance Standards apply to projects financed by IFC and are adopted by
> many lenders (e.g. Equator Principles financial institutions). They are binding on a
> project only through financing agreements, never as national legislation.

## Performance Standard 1 - Assessment and management of environmental and social risks and impacts
Clients establish an environmental and social assessment and management system,
including identification of risks and impacts, management programmes applying the
mitigation hierarchy (anticipate and avoid, minimise, compensate or offset),
organisational capacity, emergency preparedness, stakeholder engagement, a
grievance mechanism and monitoring.
Obligation: Where lender standards apply, establish an environmental and social management system and stakeholder engagement process.

## Performance Standard 2 - Labor and working conditions
Requirements cover working conditions, non-discrimination, worker organisations,
avoidance of child and forced labour, and occupational health and safety for
workers, including contracted workers.
Obligation: Where lender standards apply, provide a safe and healthy work environment and fair working conditions.

## Performance Standard 3 - Resource efficiency and pollution prevention
Clients avoid or minimise pollution, apply resource efficiency (energy, water), reduce
greenhouse gas emissions, and manage wastes and hazardous materials, applying
technically and financially feasible measures consistent with the EHS Guidelines.
Obligation: Where lender standards apply, apply pollution prevention, water and energy efficiency, and GHG quantification.

## Performance Standard 4 - Community health, safety and security
Clients evaluate risks to the health and safety of affected communities, including
infrastructure and equipment safety, hazardous materials, ecosystem services,
community exposure to disease, emergency preparedness and security personnel.
Obligation: Where lender standards apply, assess and manage risks to community health and safety.

## Performance Standard 5 - Land acquisition and involuntary resettlement
Clients avoid or minimise displacement, provide compensation at full replacement
cost, improve or restore livelihoods and living standards of displaced persons, and
prepare a resettlement action plan or livelihood restoration plan where needed.
Obligation: Where lender standards apply, avoid displacement and prepare resettlement or livelihood restoration plans.

## Performance Standard 6 - Biodiversity conservation and sustainable management of living natural resources
Clients protect and conserve biodiversity, maintain ecosystem services, and apply
heightened requirements in natural and critical habitats, including the mitigation
hierarchy and, as a last resort, biodiversity offsets.
Obligation: Where lender standards apply, assess habitats (modified, natural, critical) and apply the mitigation hierarchy to biodiversity impacts.

## Performance Standard 8 - Cultural heritage
Clients protect cultural heritage from adverse impacts, apply a chance-finds
procedure, and consult affected communities regarding cultural heritage.
Obligation: Where lender standards apply, protect cultural heritage and apply a chance-finds procedure.
