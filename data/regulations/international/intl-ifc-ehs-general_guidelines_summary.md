# World Bank Group Environmental, Health, and Safety (EHS) General Guidelines (2007) - Curated Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. INTERNATIONAL TECHNICAL REFERENCE - NOT
> NATIONAL LAW. Guideline values apply only where required by financing agreements or
> adopted by the national regulator; national standards take precedence where more stringent.

## Section 1.1 - Air emissions and ambient air quality
Projects with significant emission sources should prevent or minimise emissions and
should not result in pollutant concentrations that reach or exceed relevant ambient
air quality guidelines and standards. Fugitive dust from construction, materials
handling and haul roads should be controlled.
Obligation: Where applied, control point-source and fugitive emissions, including dust, against ambient air quality guidelines.

## Section 1.3 - Wastewater and ambient water quality
Discharges of process wastewater, sanitary wastewater and stormwater should meet
applicable effluent guideline values and should not result in contaminant
concentrations in excess of local ambient water quality criteria.
Obligation: Where applied, treat wastewater to guideline values and protect receiving water quality.

## Section 1.5 - Hazardous materials management
Hazardous materials should be stored, handled and used so as to prevent releases,
including secondary containment (bunding) of storage tanks and spill response plans.
Obligation: Where applied, provide secondary containment and spill response for hazardous materials and fuel storage.

## Section 1.6 - Waste management
Waste management should follow the waste hierarchy and include waste segregation,
storage, transport and disposal in permitted facilities.
Obligation: Where applied, implement a waste management plan following the waste hierarchy.

## Section 1.7 - Noise
Noise impacts should not exceed guideline levels at the nearest receptor off-site,
or result in an increase of more than 3 dB above background. The guideline levels
for residential, institutional and educational receptors are 55 dB(A) during the
day and 45 dB(A) at night (one-hour LAeq).
Obligation: Where applied, assess noise at the nearest residential receptor against the guideline levels.

## Section 4 - Construction and decommissioning
Construction-phase measures address noise and vibration, soil erosion and sediment
control, dust, hazardous materials, traffic safety and community exposure, and
occupational health and safety of construction workers.
Obligation: Where applied, implement construction-phase erosion, dust, noise and traffic controls.
