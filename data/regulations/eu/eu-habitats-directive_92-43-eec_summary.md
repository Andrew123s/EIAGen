# Council Directive 92/43/EEC on the conservation of natural habitats and of wild fauna and flora (Habitats Directive) - Curated Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Only the text published in the Official
> Journal of the European Union is authoritative. Applied through national law
> (for example Sections 31-36 and 44 BNatSchG in Germany).

## Article 6(3) - Appropriate assessment
Any plan or project not directly connected with or necessary to the management of a
Natura 2000 site but likely to have a significant effect thereon, either
individually or in combination with other plans or projects, shall be subject to an
appropriate assessment of its implications for the site in view of the site's
conservation objectives. The competent authorities agree to the project only after
having ascertained that it will not adversely affect the integrity of the site.
Obligation: Screen for likely significant effects on Natura 2000 sites and carry out an appropriate assessment where such effects cannot be excluded.

## Article 6(4) - Imperative reasons of overriding public interest
If, despite a negative assessment and in the absence of alternative solutions, a
project must be carried out for imperative reasons of overriding public interest,
the Member State shall take all compensatory measures necessary to ensure that the
overall coherence of Natura 2000 is protected.
Obligation: Where Article 6(4) is invoked, demonstrate absence of alternatives and provide compensatory measures.

## Article 12 - Strict protection of species
Member States establish a system of strict protection for the animal species listed
in Annex IV, prohibiting deliberate capture or killing, deliberate disturbance (in
particular during breeding, rearing, hibernation and migration) and deterioration or
destruction of breeding sites or resting places.
Obligation: Assess effects on Annex IV species, such as bats, and avoid prohibited impacts.
