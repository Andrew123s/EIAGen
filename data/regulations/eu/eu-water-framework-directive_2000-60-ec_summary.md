# Directive 2000/60/EC establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy (Water Framework Directive) - Curated Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Only the text published in the Official
> Journal of the European Union is authoritative. Applied through national law
> (for example Sections 27-31 and 47 WHG in Germany).

## Article 4 - Environmental objectives
Member States implement the measures necessary to prevent deterioration of the
status of all bodies of surface water and groundwater, and aim to achieve good
surface water status (ecological and chemical) and good groundwater status
(quantitative and chemical).
Obligation: Demonstrate that project water abstraction, discharges or hydromorphological changes do not cause deterioration of water body status.

## Article 4(7) - New modifications
Failure to achieve good status or to prevent deterioration resulting from new
modifications to the physical characteristics of a surface water body, or alterations
to the level of groundwater bodies, is permitted only under strict conditions,
including overriding public interest and the absence of better environmental options.
Obligation: Where a project modifies a water body (e.g. impoundment), assess against the Article 4(7) conditions.

## Article 11 - Programmes of measures
Programmes of measures include controls over the abstraction of fresh surface water
and groundwater (including registers and prior authorisation) and prior regulation
or authorisation of point-source discharges liable to cause pollution.
Obligation: Expect prior authorisation requirements for water abstraction and point-source discharges.
