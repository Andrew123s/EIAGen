# Bundes-Immissionsschutzgesetz (BImSchG) - Federal Immission Control Act - Curated English Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Unofficial English summary of selected
> provisions of the BImSchG (as promulgated on 17 May 2013, as subsequently amended).
> Only the German text is authoritative.

## Section 4 - Permit requirement for installations
The construction and operation of installations which, owing to their nature or
operation, are particularly likely to cause harmful effects on the environment
(air pollution, noise, vibration and similar) require a permit. The installations
requiring a permit are specified in the Fourth Ordinance (4. BImSchV), by type and
capacity threshold.
Obligation: Obtain an immission-control permit where the installation is listed in the 4. BImSchV.
Permit: Immission-control permit (BImSchG-Genehmigung)

## Section 5 - Obligations of operators of installations requiring a permit
Installations requiring a permit must be constructed and operated so that harmful
environmental effects and other hazards are not caused, precautions are taken
against harmful effects (in particular by state-of-the-art emission reduction),
waste is avoided, recovered or disposed of properly, and energy is used efficiently.
Obligation: Apply state-of-the-art emission control and waste and energy obligations for permitted installations.

## Section 10 - Permit procedure
The permit procedure for installations requiring a formal procedure includes an
application with plans and descriptions, public announcement, display of the
documents, the opportunity for objections and a discussion date. Section 19 provides
a simplified procedure without public participation for listed installations.
Obligation: Follow the formal or simplified permit procedure as determined by the 4. BImSchV listing.

## Section 48 - Technical instructions (TA Luft, TA Lärm)
Administrative regulations such as the Technical Instructions on Air Quality Control
(TA Luft) and on Noise Abatement (TA Lärm) specify emission and immission values
used in the assessment of installations.
Obligation: Assess air emissions and noise against TA Luft and TA Lärm values where applicable.
