# Gesetz über die Umweltverträglichkeitsprüfung (UVPG) - Environmental Impact Assessment Act - Curated English Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Unofficial English summary of selected
> provisions of the UVPG (as promulgated on 18 March 2021, as subsequently amended).
> Only the German text published on gesetze-im-internet.de / in the Federal Law
> Gazette is authoritative. Annex 1 rows other than those summarised here must be
> read from the official text.

## Section 2 - Protected assets (Schutzgüter)
The environmental impact assessment identifies, describes and assesses the direct
and indirect effects of a project on: human beings, in particular human health;
animals, plants and biological diversity; land, soil, water, air, climate and
landscape; cultural heritage and other material assets; and the interaction between
these protected assets.
Obligation: Assess project effects on all protected assets, including human health, biodiversity, soil, water, air, climate, landscape and cultural heritage.

## Section 5 - Determination of the EIA obligation
The competent authority determines whether an EIA obligation exists, on the
application of the project developer or on its own initiative, on the basis of the
information provided by the developer and its own information. The determination
is made public.
Obligation: Request or await the competent authority's determination of whether an EIA is required.

## Section 6 - Mandatory EIA for new projects
An EIA is mandatory for a new project listed in Annex 1 and marked with an "X" in
column 1, where the size or performance values stated there are reached or exceeded.
Obligation: Carry out an EIA where the project is an Annex 1 column 1 ("X") project.
Permit: Development consent following EIA

## Section 7 - Preliminary assessment of the individual case for new projects
For new projects marked "A" in column 2 of Annex 1, the competent authority carries
out a general preliminary assessment of the individual case (allgemeine Vorprüfung);
for projects marked "S", a site-related preliminary assessment (standortbezogene
Vorprüfung). An EIA is required if the project may have significant adverse
environmental effects, taking into account the criteria in Annex 3.
Obligation: Provide information for the preliminary assessment of the individual case where the project is an Annex 1 "A" or "S" project.

## Section 15 - Scoping
On request of the developer, or if the authority considers it appropriate, the
competent authority advises the developer on the content, scope and level of detail
of the EIA report (Untersuchungsrahmen), and may consult other authorities.
Obligation: Seek scoping advice on the content and scope of the EIA report where appropriate.

## Section 16 - EIA report (UVP-Bericht)
The developer submits an EIA report containing at least: a description of the
project; a description of the environment and its components in the project area;
the features of the project and its site and measures to avoid, reduce or offset
significant adverse effects; a description of the expected significant
environmental effects; a description of the reasonable alternatives examined; and a
non-technical summary. Further details follow from Annex 4.
Obligation: Prepare an EIA report with the content required by Section 16 and Annex 4.

## Annex 1, No. 1.6 - Wind farms
Installations for the use of wind energy with a total height of more than 50 metres
(wind farms) are listed in Annex 1, No. 1.6: No. 1.6.1 - 20 or more wind turbines
(column X, mandatory EIA); No. 1.6.2 - 6 to fewer than 20 wind turbines (column A,
general preliminary assessment); No. 1.6.3 - 3 to fewer than 6 wind turbines
(column S, site-related preliminary assessment).
Obligation: Determine the applicable Annex 1 No. 1.6 row from the number and height of wind turbines.

## Annex 1, No. 2.1 - Quarries
Quarrying with an extraction area of 25 hectares or more is listed in Annex 1,
No. 2.1.1 (column X, mandatory EIA). Smaller extraction areas are listed in
subsequent rows subject to preliminary assessment; those rows must be read from
the official text.
Obligation: Determine the applicable Annex 1 No. 2.1 row from the extraction area.

## Annex 3 - Criteria for the preliminary assessment
Criteria include the characteristics of the project (size, cumulation with other
projects, use of natural resources, waste generation, pollution and nuisance, risk
of accidents), the location of the project (existing use, quality and regenerative
capacity of natural resources, and the protection criteria of the area, such as
Natura 2000 sites, nature reserves, national parks, biosphere reserves, protected
biotopes, water protection areas and areas of high population density) and the type
and characteristics of the potential effects.
Obligation: Address Annex 3 criteria in the information supplied for the preliminary assessment.
