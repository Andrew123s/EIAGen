# Wasserhaushaltsgesetz (WHG) - Federal Water Act - Curated English Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Unofficial English summary of selected
> provisions of the WHG (of 31 July 2009, as subsequently amended). Only the German
> text is authoritative. Land (state) water laws supplement the WHG.

## Section 8 - Permit or authorisation required for water uses
The use of a water body requires a permit (Erlaubnis) or an authorisation
(Bewilligung), unless the Act provides otherwise.
Obligation: Obtain a water-law permit or authorisation for water uses.
Permit: Water-law permit (Erlaubnis) or authorisation (Bewilligung)

## Section 9 - Water uses
Water uses include the withdrawal and diversion of water from surface waters,
damming and lowering of surface waters, the introduction and discharge of
substances into water bodies, and the withdrawal, extraction, lifting and diversion
of groundwater (water abstraction), as well as measures likely to cause significant
adverse changes in water quality.
Obligation: Identify all project activities that constitute water uses (abstraction, discharge, impoundment).

## Section 27 - Management objectives for surface waters
Surface waters are to be managed so as to avoid deterioration of their ecological
and chemical status and to maintain or achieve good ecological and chemical status.
Obligation: Demonstrate that the project does not cause deterioration of surface water status.

## Section 47 - Management objectives for groundwater
Groundwater is to be managed so as to avoid deterioration of its quantitative and
chemical status, to reverse significant upward trends in pollutant concentrations,
and to maintain or achieve good quantitative and chemical status.
Obligation: Demonstrate that groundwater abstraction or discharges do not cause deterioration of groundwater status.

## Section 57 - Discharge of wastewater into water bodies
A permit for the discharge of wastewater into water bodies may only be granted if
the quantity and harmfulness of the wastewater are kept as low as possible in
accordance with the state of the art, the discharge is compatible with the
requirements for the water body, and the necessary wastewater facilities are built
and operated.
Obligation: Treat wastewater to the state of the art before discharge and obtain a discharge permit.
Permit: Direct discharge permit
