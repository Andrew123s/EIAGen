# Bundesnaturschutzgesetz (BNatSchG) - Federal Nature Conservation Act - Curated English Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Unofficial English summary of selected
> provisions of the BNatSchG (of 29 July 2009, as subsequently amended). Only the
> German text is authoritative. Land nature conservation laws supplement it.

## Section 14 - Interventions in nature and landscape
Interventions are changes in the shape or use of land, or changes in the
groundwater level connected with the living soil layer, that may significantly
impair the functional capacity of the natural balance or the landscape scenery.
Land clearing, excavation and construction of infrastructure outside built-up
areas typically constitute interventions.
Obligation: Assess whether land clearing, earthworks or construction constitute an intervention in nature and landscape.

## Section 15 - Obligations of the party causing the intervention
The party causing an intervention must avoid avoidable impairments of nature and
landscape. Unavoidable impairments must be compensated (Ausgleich) or replaced
(Ersatz) by measures of nature conservation and landscape management. Where this is
not possible, a substitute payment may be required.
Obligation: Avoid impairments and compensate or replace unavoidable impairments of nature and landscape (mitigation hierarchy).

## Section 30 - Legally protected biotopes
Actions that may destroy or otherwise significantly impair certain biotopes (for
example natural watercourses and floodplains, wetlands, reed beds, dry grasslands
and certain forests) are prohibited, subject to exceptions.
Obligation: Identify legally protected biotopes in the project area and avoid their impairment.

## Section 34 - Compatibility with Natura 2000 sites
Before being permitted or carried out, projects that, individually or in
combination with other projects, are likely to significantly affect a Natura 2000
site (Special Area of Conservation or Special Protection Area) must be assessed for
compatibility with the conservation objectives of that site.
Obligation: Carry out a Natura 2000 compatibility (pre-)assessment where a Natura 2000 site may be affected.

## Section 44 - Species protection prohibitions
It is prohibited to kill or injure wild animals of specially protected species, to
significantly disturb strictly protected species and European bird species during
breeding, rearing, moulting, hibernation and migration periods, and to damage or
destroy their breeding or resting sites.
Obligation: Assess species protection (artenschutzrechtliche Prüfung), including birds and bats, and avoid prohibited effects.
