# Kreislaufwirtschaftsgesetz (KrWG) - Circular Economy Act - Curated English Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Unofficial English summary of selected
> provisions of the KrWG (of 24 February 2012, as subsequently amended). Only the
> German text is authoritative.

## Section 6 - Waste hierarchy
Measures for the avoidance and management of waste follow the order of priority:
prevention; preparation for re-use; recycling; other recovery, in particular energy
recovery and backfilling; and disposal.
Obligation: Apply the waste hierarchy to construction and operational waste streams.

## Section 7 - Obligations of waste producers and holders - recovery
Producers or holders of waste are obliged to recover their waste; recovery takes
priority over disposal where technically possible and economically reasonable.
Obligation: Recover construction and operational waste where technically and economically feasible.

## Section 15 - Obligations of waste producers and holders - disposal
Waste that is not recovered must be disposed of in a manner that does not impair the
public interest, in particular human health, animals, plants, water and soil.
Obligation: Dispose of non-recoverable waste through authorised facilities.
