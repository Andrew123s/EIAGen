# Water Resources Commission Act, 1996 (Act 522) and Water Use Regulations, 2001 (L.I. 1692) - Curated Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Section references, exemptions and fees
> must be verified against the official Act and Regulations.

## Act 522 - Water Resources Commission
The Act establishes the Water Resources Commission as the body responsible for the
regulation and management of the utilisation of water resources, and for the
coordination of policy in relation to water resources in Ghana. Property in and
control of all water resources is vested in the President on behalf of the people.

## Act 522, section 13 - Use of water resources requires authorisation
A person shall not divert, dam, store, abstract or use water resources, or construct
or maintain any works for the use of water resources, except in accordance with the
Act. Water rights are granted by the Commission.
Obligation: Obtain authorisation from the Water Resources Commission before abstracting, impounding or using water resources.
Permit: Water use permit / water right

## L.I. 1692 - Water use permits
The Water Use Regulations require a water use permit for the use of water,
including abstraction from surface water or groundwater, for purposes such as
domestic use above exempt levels, commercial, industrial, agricultural, mining,
power generation and recreational uses. Applications are made to the Commission and
may be subject to conditions and fees. Certain domestic and small-scale uses are exempt.
Obligation: Apply for a water use permit for water abstraction or other water use not covered by an exemption.
Permit: Water use permit

## Discharge of wastewater into water bodies
Discharge of effluent or wastewater into water bodies is a regulated use of water
resources and is subject to authorisation and to applicable effluent quality
requirements set by the environmental regulator.
Obligation: Obtain authorisation for wastewater discharge and meet applicable effluent standards.
Permit: Water use permit (discharge); environmental permit conditions
