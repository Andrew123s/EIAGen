## Section 2 - Functions of the Agency
The Act established the Environmental Protection Agency with functions that include
advising the Minister on environmental policy, coordinating environmental activities,
issuing environmental permits and pollution abatement notices, prescribing
standards and guidelines for the protection of the environment, and ensuring
compliance with laid-down environmental impact assessment procedures in the
planning and execution of development projects.
Obligation: Development projects are subject to the environmental regulator's assessment and permitting procedures.

## Section 12 - Power to require environmental impact assessment
The Agency may, by notice in writing, require a person responsible for an
undertaking which in its opinion has or is likely to have an adverse effect on the
environment to submit an environmental impact assessment containing the
information specified in the notice.
Obligation: Submit an environmental impact assessment when required by the regulator.

## Enforcement notices
The Agency may serve an enforcement notice where an activity is being carried out
in a manner that causes or is likely to cause serious pollution or danger to public
health, requiring the person to take specified steps.
Obligation: Comply with enforcement notices issued by the regulator.
