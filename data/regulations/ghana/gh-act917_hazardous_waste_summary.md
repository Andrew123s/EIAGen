# Hazardous and Electronic Waste Control and Management Act, 2016 (Act 917) - Curated Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. Verify provisions and any implementing
> regulations against the official Act.

## Control of hazardous waste
The Act provides for the control, management and disposal of hazardous waste,
electrical and electronic waste and other wastes, and implements Ghana's obligations
relating to transboundary movements of hazardous wastes.
Obligation: Manage hazardous wastes and chemicals generated by the undertaking in accordance with the Act and associated permits.

## Permits for handling hazardous waste
Persons who generate, store, transport, treat or dispose of hazardous waste are
subject to permitting requirements administered by the environmental regulator.
Obligation: Obtain permits for storage, transport, treatment or disposal of hazardous wastes where required.
Permit: Hazardous waste permit
