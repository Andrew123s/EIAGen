# Minerals and Mining Act, 2006 (Act 703) - Curated Summary

> CURATED SUMMARY - NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT. The Act has been amended since 2006;
> amendments have not been verified for this summary.

## Section 9 - Requirement for a mineral right
A person shall not conduct reconnaissance, prospecting, exploration or mining for a
mineral in Ghana, or on land in Ghana, unless that person has been granted a mineral
right in accordance with the Act.
Obligation: Obtain the appropriate mineral right (reconnaissance licence, prospecting licence or mining lease) before conducting mining activities.
Permit: Mineral right / mining lease

## Mining lease applications
Applications for a mining lease are made to the Minister through the Minerals
Commission and include, among other things, a programme of mining operations and
information on the estimated ore reserves. The grant of a mining lease is distinct
from the environmental permit, which must be obtained from the environmental regulator.
Obligation: Apply for a mining lease and obtain the separate environmental permit.

## Environmental obligations of mineral right holders
Holders of mineral rights are required to comply with applicable environmental laws,
including the environmental assessment regulations, and to undertake reclamation of
land affected by mining operations.
Obligation: Comply with environmental assessment requirements and plan for reclamation and mine closure.

## Surface rights and compensation
The owner or lawful occupier of land subject to a mineral right is entitled to
compensation for disturbance of rights and damage to crops, buildings and works
caused by mining operations.
Obligation: Assess and pay compensation to land owners and occupiers affected by mining operations.
